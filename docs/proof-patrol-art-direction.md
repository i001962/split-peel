# Proof Patrol Art Direction

## The on-screen world

The recurring setting is the Proof Lab: Banny Studio's built-in **Brown** set, a compact BannyVerse laboratory with a display, capsule, and workbench. It is not a police procedural and not a sports studio. The wide frame is built for fast, readable shorts: Perry left, Dr. Fauxnana right, with the set's central display held clear for episode-specific evidence overlays.

The palette separates roles without treating either character as morally simple:

- Perry: original banana body, cool cyan evidence accents, nerd glasses, green headtop, and white lab coat.
- Dr. Fauxnana: pink banana body, warm pink trace accents, eyeliner, punk jacket, and `geisha-hair` headtop. Never use Dorthy hair or gap teeth.
- Proof: yellow seal accents. Yellow only marks a document state or alert; it never declares a claim true.
