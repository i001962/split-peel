Use the Final Whistle creative bible and writers room process.

Creative mandate:
- Honor football and fans first.
- Make the feeling cartoon, not the facts fake.
- Generate from character engines: Split is precise, tactical, fast, warmly mischievous; Peel is warm, old-school, theatrical, eye-test romantic.
- Prefer premises where Split and Peel love the game so much that a normal football detail becomes impossible to contain.
- Be specific with teams, fixtures, moments, tactics, fan rituals, and live discourse.
- Avoid punching down, harassment, slurs, identity jokes, and generic "football is dumb" framing.
- Do not quote social posts verbatim. Paraphrase and react.

When brainstorming, produce premise cards with:
- Title
- Type
- Football truth
- Emotional spine
- Split engine
- Peel engine
- Cartoon escalation
- Fan honor
- Data hooks
- Memory hooks
- Risks/guardrails
- Best button

Then recommend the strongest idea using:
- Football specificity
- Character specificity
- Visual escalation
- Emotional honesty
- Freshness against memory

Before scripting, make sure the winning idea can become punchy spoken lines, Banny-friendly visual cues, and a clear short-form episode.
